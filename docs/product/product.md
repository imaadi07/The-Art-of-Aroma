# Product Overview: THE ART OF AROMA

## Identity & Aesthetic
THE ART OF AROMA is an ultra-luxury digital web experience celebrating the Japanese Koh-do incense tradition of Awaji Island.
Palette: Charcoal Noir (`#151410`), Warm Ivory (`#F2EEE5`), Muted Grey (`#9E9A90`), Antique Brass (`#C5A880`), Ember Orange (`#FF7814`), Stone Charcoal (`#302D26`).
Editorial Typography: Cormorant Garamond & Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Page Journey & 3D Choreography

### 1. Main Preloader
- Volumetric silky smoke puff rises from bottom center into the screen.
- Smoke coalesces into the brand seal and "THE ART OF AROMA" typography.
- Holds for 0.5 seconds in stillness.
- Zooms fullscreen with soft golden/atmospheric blur, then dissolves away revealing the Hero.

### 2. Hero Section (Pinned Scrubbed Reveal)
- Incense stick burns with steady, silky smoke and active ember flicker.
- Page stays pinned while user scrolls down.
- Editorial headline and narrative emerge directly from the burning flame tip (scaling from 0.04 to 1.0, unblurring from 16px to 0px).
- Fully reversible: scrolling up shrinks text back into the flame.

### 3. Page 2: About the Company
- Stick glides with scroll towards the right side.
- 3D ceremonial brass holder rises up.
- When Page 2 is full screen, the stick plants into the ash well of the brass holder on the right.
- Left side displays the company's Awaji Island heritage story, purity philosophy, and provenance metrics (1876 Foundation, 100% Tabu-no-ki bark, 45 Min burn).

### 4. Page 3: Products Offered (Revolving Orbit)
- Master stick and brass holder glide to the center of the revolving orbit as their final position.
- 4 formulation sticks orbit in 3D around the center.
- Pure typography on screen (no cards or background boxes).
- Text dissolves during stick transitions.
- Staggered information placement:
  - N° 01 (Kyara Woods): Top Left
  - N° 02 (Hinoki Mist): Bottom Left
  - N° 03 (Sumi & Shiso): Top Right
  - N° 04 (Byakudan Sandalwood): Bottom Right
- Dynamic golden-glowing curvy Bézier spline connects from the burning ember of the front stick directly to the text block.
- In between the revolving sticks in the center floats the 3D luxury packet packaging.
- Supports instant upload/drop-in of custom reference packet image.

### 5. Page 4: Contact
- Clean 2-column layout:
  - Left side: One simple museum-grade photograph of the Kyoto sanctuary.
  - Right side: Minimal luxury correspondence form with subtle, calm focus animations.
